AC Current and DC Current
Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor or electrical circuit.
Based on how current flows and changes with time, we mainly classify it into two types:
AC – Alternating Current
DC – Direct Current
Let's understand both from the basics. 😊
1. 🔋 What is DC Current?
DC stands for Direct Current.
DC is an electric current that flows in only one direction.
Current direction remains the same.
Polarity remains the same.
Its magnitude can be constant or vary with time.
Commonly supplied by batteries and DC power supplies.
Examples:
🔋 Mobile phone batteries
🔌 12 V DC power supply
⚙️ 24 V DC PLC control circuits
💻 Electronic circuits

2. 🏠 What is AC Current?
AC stands for Alternating Current.
AC is an electric current that periodically changes its direction and usually its magnitude.
Current direction changes periodically.
Voltage polarity changes periodically.
Commonly used in homes and industries.
Produced by alternators and generators.
Examples:
🏠 Household electricity
🏭 Industrial three-phase power supply
⚙️ AC induction motors
💡 Electrical appliances
Example:
In India, household AC electricity has a nominal frequency of 50 Hz.
This means the current completes 50 full cycles every second.
